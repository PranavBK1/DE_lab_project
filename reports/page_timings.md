## Known issue: (d) for blockbusters on a cold cache

Page (d) for a movie with tens of thousands of ratings reads that movie's ratings from about 53,000 pages of the 1.2 GB `ratings` table, which this laptop cannot keep in memory. A cold load takes 8–10 s; once loaded, the page is served from `st.cache_data` in under 0.1 s for 10 minutes, or until the next rating change clears the cache. The fix options are listed in [query_performance.md](query_performance.md) and await approval.
